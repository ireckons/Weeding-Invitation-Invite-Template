## 🚀 Running on Localhost

Whenever you want to run it locally, simply double-click:
* **`start_server.bat`** (Automatically launches the server and opens your browser at [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000))
* Or run in terminal: `python server.py`

## 🎨 How to Customize Your Invitation

1. **Background Music:**
   - Replace `assets/audio/music.mp3` with any wedding song of your choice (keep the filename `music.mp3`).

2. **Couple Photos & Images:**
   - Look in `assets/images/` to swap couple photos, cover portraits, or gallery pictures with your own photos.

3. **Text & Details (Names, Date, Events, Venue):**
   - Open `index.html` in an editor to customize the names of the bride and groom, wedding itinerary (Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception), Google Maps venue link, and RSVP form.
